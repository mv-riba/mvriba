Scope Creeps

"Scope creep" is what happens when a project quietly grows beyond its brief. So is a career.

Hosts Aleksi and Marija — a developer who keeps drifting into product, and a marketer who drifted into it for good — talk about the ever-expanding overlap of product, engineering, design, and AI. Honest, curious conversations about how building software actually works now, and where it's heading next.
