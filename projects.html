02 Personal finance Next.js · Postgres

Worthwise

A household net worth tracker. Once a month, check in your bank accounts, savings, pensions, property, mortgages and crypto in any currency, then watch your combined net worth grow, with no bank connections.

Try Worthwise ↗ View code

Every account, every currency

Balances are converted at each month's European Central Bank rate, so past totals stay accurate.

Made for two

Each partner has their own login. Joint items are counted once and split by ownership share, and you only ever see your partner's total.

Property that keeps itself current

Home values move with the official house price index for UK and Finnish areas, and mortgages count against your equity.

Private by design

Manual entry only, no bank credentials, and row-level security in the database so each person sees only what's theirs.
