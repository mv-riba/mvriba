About me

A global, multidisciplinary perspective on product

I grew up in Riga, Latvia. Since then, I've studied and worked across North America and Europe, from the USA and Canada to the UK, Denmark and now Finland. Moving that often taught me to adapt quickly, work across cultures and listen for what people actually need. It turns out those are also the core skills of product management.

My first industry roles were in PR and marketing for global payments. The closer I worked with the engineers and designers building our products, the more I wanted to understand how good products get made. At the crypto exchange Wirex, I got the chance to move into product management, and I haven't looked back.

Since then I've led products in crypto, fintech, Web3 art certification and, now, AI-powered B2B SaaS. My marketing background still shapes how I work. I start with the customer, and I've learned that great marketing can't rescue a weak product, while a strong product still needs a clear story.

How I work

01

Users first, technology second

Most users don't care what's under the hood. They care about convenience, ease and simplicity, so the technology should stay in the background.

02

Commercially grounded

Every roadmap item needs a reason for the customer and a reason for the business. Pricing and packaging are product decisions too.

03

Predictable delivery

I've introduced ShapeUp and structured release processes at my current and previous companies so that teams can commit to dates and meet them.

04

Build it yourself

I use AI agents in my own work, from shipping UI changes to building my own apps, so I know first-hand what these tools can and can't do.
