Gentle by design

The months after birth are not the time for diets or pressure. Momig is built to help you look after yourself, not to judge you.

No suggested targetsMomig never tells you how much to eat. If you want a calorie goal, it’s your own number, ideally agreed with your midwife or doctor.
No “bounce back”No weight tracking, no deficits and no red or green marks on what you ate.
Kind notes, only if you askWith a goal set, you can switch on a short note on Today. It’s a nudge, never a warning, and it’s off with Hide numbers.
Rest counts tooDays with nothing logged are fine. There are no streaks and nothing to catch up on.
Momig is a personal log, not medical advice. It can’t diagnose anything. For anything about your recovery, feeding or mood, talk to your midwife or doctor.
