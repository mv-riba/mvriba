From the maker of Bebig

Bebig started as a messy shared note of feeds and naps in the first weeks with a newborn. Somewhere in all that tracking, there was no room for the person doing the feeding. Momig is that missing half: the same calm, one-tap design, pointed at you.

MarijaFounder, MVR Studios

Made with care

The months after birth look different for everyone, whether you’re recovering, breastfeeding or simply exhausted. Momig never uses weight-loss language, never sets a target for you and never comments on your body.

For anything about your recovery, feeding or mood, talk to your midwife or doctor, or bring it up at your check-ups.
